### Tell me about yourself
I actually came into CS  from more of a business background, and over the past couple of years, a lot of my CS experience has come through internships.
I've worked across full-stack development, AI backend, and more recently backend and infrastructure as a platform engineer.

But because I've spent so much time on internships, recruiting, and school, I haven't been as involved in the CS community on campus.
So this year I really wanted to change that, meet more people in CS and contribute to something on campus as well.

### Why WiCS
Most of the peers I know are either people I've worked/teamed with, and I realized I actually don't know that many girls in CS at UofT.
One of the biggest reasons I wanted to join is honestly just to become more involved in the community.
I'd love to meet more girls who are interested in similar things, building somthing together, and have poeplke to  talk to about school, internships, or tech in general.
I also like the **mentorship** side. I lot of people helped me when I was first getting into CS, so now I'd really like to give some of that back.

### Get out of this experience.
The biggest thing for me is probably the people. I'd really like to meet more girls in CS who are at a similar stage.
I think what I'm looking for here is actually pretty differnt from what I look ofr in an internship.
With internships, I'm very focused on learning professionally and becoming a better engineer.
But for the community, what excites me more is getting to build something with other students and contributing to a community that I actually want to be part of.
Ownership.
Backend is just where I feel I can contribute the most.

### Why now
If I'm not already part of a community, I probably won't naturally go to that many events on my own, especially when shool and recruting get busy.
But once I acutally know people there, it feels different. It's just easier when you know people and somone text you, are you coming tonight, then you acutally go.
