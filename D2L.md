### Why D2L?

One reason I'm interested in D2L is that I'd like to get back to working more directly on a product team.

My most recent internship was very platform-focused. I worked on data ingestion and backend infrastructure, and I really enjoyed the technical depth of that work. But for my next internship, I'd also like to broaden my experience and work closer to the actual product and users again.

That's something that stood out to me about this role. From what I read, co-ops actually contribute to Brightspace and work on real team deliverables, rather than separate intern projects. 

And as a student myself, education software is also a product space that's very easy for me to relate to.
